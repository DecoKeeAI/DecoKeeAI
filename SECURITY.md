# Security Policy

If you believe you have found a security issue related to DK-Suite or DECOKEE Quake, please do not disclose it publicly.

Please contact DECOKEE through our official support channel.

DK-Suite is the official software for DECOKEE Quake and is maintained separately from the open-source DecoKeeAI concept project.

Third-party forks, plugins, scripts, or community projects are not covered by DECOKEE official support unless explicitly announced by DECOKEE.
